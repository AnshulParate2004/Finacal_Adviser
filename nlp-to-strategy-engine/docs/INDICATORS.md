## Supported Technical Indicators

---

### Simple Moving Average (SMA)

**Description:**  
Calculates the average price over a fixed number of periods.

**Use case:**  
Identifies overall price trend and smooths price fluctuations.

**Formula:**  
SMA = (Sum of last N prices) / N

---

### Exponential Moving Average (EMA)

**Description:**  
A moving average that gives more weight to recent prices.

**Use case:**  
Faster reaction to recent price changes compared to SMA.

---

### Relative Strength Index (RSI)

**Description:**  
Momentum oscillator that measures speed and change of price movements.

**Range:**  
0 to 100

**Use case:**  
- RSI > 70 → Overbought  
- RSI < 30 → Oversold

---

### Moving Average Convergence Divergence (MACD)

**Description:**  
Measures the relationship between two exponential moving averages.

**Components:**
- MACD Line
- Signal Line
- Histogram

**Use case:**  
Detects trend direction and momentum shifts.

---

### Bollinger Bands

**Description:**  
Volatility bands placed above and below a moving average.

**Components:**
- Upper Band
- Middle Band (Moving Average)
- Lower Band

**Use case:**  
Identifies price volatility and potential breakouts.

---

### Average True Range (ATR)

**Description:**  
Measures market volatility using high, low, and close prices.

**Use case:**  
Determines stop-loss levels and volatility-based position sizing.

---

### Average Directional Index (ADX)

**Description:**  
Measures the strength of a trend, not its direction.

**Use case:**  
- Low ADX → Weak or sideways trend  
- High ADX → Strong trend

---

### Stochastic Oscillator

**Description:**  
Momentum indicator comparing a closing price to its price range over a period.

**Components:**
- %K line
- %D line (smoothed)

**Use case:**  
Identifies overbought and oversold market conditions.

---
